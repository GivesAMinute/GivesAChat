GivesAChat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 August 2026

GivesAChat is a personal live-streaming tool. It collects live chat messages from the streaming platforms its operator broadcasts to and displays them together in a single on-screen overlay, so that a streamer can read comments from several platforms at once.

It is operated by a single individual for their own broadcasts. It is not a commercial service, has no user accounts, and is not offered to the public.

What information is processed

When the operator is live, GivesAChat reads the public chat attached to that broadcast. For each message this is:

  • the commenter's public display name
  • the commenter's public profile picture
  • the text of the comment, and any emote or sticker in it
  • the time the comment was posted
  • public badges or roles shown alongside the comment

This is the same information any other viewer of the stream can see in the chat window. Nothing private is requested, and no information is read from anyone who has not chosen to comment on the broadcast.

What it is used for

Solely to display the message on screen during the broadcast, for the duration it is on screen. Messages are passed straight through to the overlay as they arrive.

What is stored

Chat messages are not stored. They are relayed to the overlay in real time and discarded. Closing the overlay ends the connection and nothing is written to disk or to a database.

The only data retained is what is needed to connect to each platform:

  • access tokens belonging to the operator's own accounts, held as encrypted secrets in Cloudflare Workers
  • the operator's own channel and broadcast identifiers

No commenter data is retained after a message leaves the screen.

What is not done

  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with third parties
  • No advertising, tracking pixels, or analytics
  • No profiling, and no building of any record of individuals
  • No collection of email addresses, phone numbers, or contacts
  • No access to private messages, friends lists, or anything not publicly attached to a comment

Third parties

Chat is read from the platforms the operator broadcasts to, using each platform's own public API. Those platforms handle your data under their own privacy policies. GivesAChat runs on Cloudflare Workers, which processes the traffic in transit.

User data deletion

Because chat messages are never stored, there is normally nothing to delete: a message ceases to exist in this system the moment it leaves the overlay.

If you have commented on one of the operator's broadcasts and want to confirm that no record of you is held, or want to ask that your comments not be displayed on future broadcasts, email the address below with the platform and the display name you used. Requests are handled within 30 days.

To remove this application's access to your own Facebook account entirely, go to Facebook → Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites, select the app, and choose Remove.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly process information from anyone under 13.
